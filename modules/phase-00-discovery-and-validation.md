# Phase 00 — Discovery and Validation

## Goal

Validate that a specific customer segment has a painful, frequent, and valuable workflow problem that this product can solve better than its existing CRM, automation, and AI tools.

This phase comes directly from the research questions in `Target.md`. The gaps are hypotheses, not established differentiators.

## Modules

### M00.1 — Target customer and workflow selection

- Select one initial customer segment, such as a small B2B sales or customer-success team.
- Identify the primary workflow to improve: for example, lead handoff, post-meeting follow-up, support escalation, or renewal risk review.
- Define the users, systems, data, approvals, and business outcomes involved.
- Document who is explicitly out of scope for the first release.

### M00.2 — Customer interviews and workflow observation

- Conduct interviews with decision-makers, managers, operators, and technical administrators.
- Observe the selected workflow using real or anonymised examples.
- Record current steps, applications, handoffs, delays, duplicate work, failure modes, and trust concerns.
- Quantify frequency, time spent, cost of failure, and current workaround quality.

### M00.3 — Competitive and replacement analysis

- Compare the selected workflow against existing CRM, automation, AI assistant, and integration products.
- Identify what customers already solve adequately.
- Determine whether the opportunity is a missing capability, a poor user experience, a reliability problem, or an integration/governance problem.
- Define the smallest product advantage that would justify a trial.

### M00.4 — Outcome and evaluation design

- Define a baseline for time-to-complete, task completion, response time, errors, rework, and human intervention.
- Define what evidence an AI response must show to be trusted.
- Define acceptable and unacceptable autonomous actions.
- Create a test set of representative cases, including incomplete, conflicting, and sensitive data.

## Deliverables

- Validated target customer profile.
- One prioritised workflow and service blueprint.
- Problem evidence matrix covering frequency, impact, and existing alternatives.
- Competitive comparison and differentiation hypothesis.
- MVP outcome metrics and evaluation dataset.
- Initial risk register and out-of-scope list.

## Dependencies

None. This phase may use prototypes or manual simulations, but it should not require a production platform.

## Exit criteria

- At least one workflow is confirmed as frequent, painful, and economically meaningful.
- At least three representative users agree to evaluate a prototype or pilot.
- The team can state the MVP workflow in one sentence.
- The team can identify which actions require approval and why.
- The team has baseline metrics and a test set for evaluating quality and reliability.

## Do not build yet

- Broad CRM parity.
- A large integration marketplace.
- General-purpose autonomous agents.
- A generic chatbot without a defined operational workflow.
